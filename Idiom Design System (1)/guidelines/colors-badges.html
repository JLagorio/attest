Never established. Not reviewed, Evidence restricted. Not the same as Not applicable.

Satisfied is the absence of a badge — a determined objective says Satisfied in muted text, so no green habit can form and no screen can imply a percentage secure. At most a third of the rows in a collection carry a badge; past that, the states are not urgent and belong in muted text too.

Neutral exists so a factual state does not have to borrow urgency. Without it, Inherited reads as attention and the screen lies about what needs a human. Every label must be readable with the tint removed — StateBadge owns the label-to-tier mapping so the same state cannot change colour between screens.
