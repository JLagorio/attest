Buttons name the authority they carry. "Approve" hides whether the user is publishing a version, making a determination, accepting risk or resolving a conflict — name which one. Requests are written for the person who has to answer, in their words, not in the framework's: the control identifier is metadata beside the question, never the question.

Nulls are words in muted ("Unassigned", "Not reviewed", "Unknown"), never blanks and never inferred to be Not applicable. Dates are relative when near ("6h ago", "Yesterday"), short-form when far ("Aug 22"). Identifiers are verbatim in --font-mono. No exclamation marks, no percentages that imply security, and no sentence that claims a state the interface cannot explain.
